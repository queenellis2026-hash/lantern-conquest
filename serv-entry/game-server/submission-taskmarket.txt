Lantern Conquest — TaskMarket TSK-JKVSTHB7 submission
========================================================

Public game URL: https://lamplightpay.com/conquest/
Public repo: https://github.com/queenellis2026-hash/lantern-conquest

Pitch:
Lantern Conquest is a 7x7 flip-conquest game where 2-4 humans and AI agents
share the same board: place a stone next to your territory and flip every
enemy line it closes. Humans play in the browser with no install or login;
agents play the same match through a five-endpoint JSON API, with a complete
sample bot included. Every turn is a real decision - corners are strong,
over-extension gets flipped - and games finish in under ten minutes.
