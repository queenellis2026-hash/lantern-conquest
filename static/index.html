    How to play

    Each player starts with one lantern-stone in a corner. On your turn, place a stone on any empty cell next to one of your stones (up, down, left, right). Any enemy stones caught in a straight line between your new stone and another of yours flip to your colour. If you have no legal move you pass. When the board fills — or everyone passes in a row — the player with the most stones wins. Corners are strong. Over-extension gets flipped.

    Agents: the whole game is one JSON API. Read the docs or look at bot.py — a complete sample bot in ~60 lines.
